# Competitors

Map the competitive landscape for: **$ARGUMENTS**

## What This Skill Does

Produce a structured competitive landscape overview — who's competing, how they're positioned, and where the open space is.

## Process

### 1. Define the Competitive Arena

- What problem space are we mapping?
- Direct competitors (same product, same customer)
- Indirect competitors (different product, same job-to-be-done)
- Potential entrants (big players who could move in)

### 2. Identify All Competitors

Generate a comprehensive list of:
- **Tier 1** — primary direct competitors (3-5)
- **Tier 2** — secondary alternatives customers consider (3-5)
- **Tier 3** — adjacent tools that partially solve the problem (3-5)

### 3. Comparison Matrix

Build a feature/attribute table:

| Competitor | Target Customer | Price Range | Key Differentiator | Biggest Weakness |
|------------|----------------|-------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| [Name]     | ...            | ...         | ...               | ...             |

Include columns relevant to $ARGUMENTS (may include: AI features, mobile app, API access, languages, integrations, free tier).

### 4. Positioning Map

Describe where each competitor sits on two key axes (choose axes relevant to the space):
- Common axes: price (cheap → premium), ease of use (simple → powerful), breadth (focused → all-in-one)
- Identify white space — positioning no one owns yet

### 5. Key Insights

- Who is the market leader and why?
- Who is growing fastest?
- What positioning is overcrowded?
- What positioning is unclaimed?
- What feature do customers wish existed that no one has built?

## Output

Deliver a competitive landscape summary, comparison matrix, positioning map description, and 3 strategic implications for the product in question.

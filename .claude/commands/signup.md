# Signup

Optimize signup flow design and copy for maximum conversion: **$ARGUMENTS**

## What This Skill Does

Reduce friction, clarify the value exchange, and write copy that converts hesitant visitors into registered users.

## Signup Flow Principles

Every field, every screen, every word in the signup flow costs conversions. Add friction only when it is worth more than the conversions it kills.

The best signup flows are:
- **Faster than expected** — fewer steps than the user anticipated
- **Trust-building** — clear about what they're getting and what you'll do with their data
- **Outcome-focused** — remind the user why they're signing up, not just what to fill in

## Page-Level Optimization

### Above the Form

- **Headline**: state the outcome they're about to get, not "Create your account"
  - "Start building in 2 minutes" beats "Sign up for [Product]"
- **Subhead (optional)**: one benefit or social proof signal
  - "Join 12,000 teams who ship faster with [product]"
- **Social proof signals**: logo strip or user count if you have it

### The Form Itself

**Fields to include**: Name, email, password. Nothing else on step 1.
- First name only (not full name) reduces abandonment
- Password rules: show them upfront, not after failed submission
- If SSO is available (Google, GitHub): make it the primary CTA, form below

**Field labels**: above the input, not inside (placeholder text disappears when typing)
**Error messages**: specific ("Enter a valid email address" not "Invalid input")
**Progress indicator**: required for multi-step flows (show step X of Y)

### Social Sign-In

When to prioritize:
- Developer / tech audience: GitHub SSO converts significantly higher
- Consumer product: Google SSO is fastest path
- B2B: Google SSO + email fallback

Button copy: "Continue with Google" not "Sign up with Google" (lower commitment framing)

### The CTA Button

- Specific verb: "Create my free account" > "Sign up" > "Submit"
- Reinforce risk reversal below: "Free forever. No credit card required."
- Color: high contrast with background, consistent with your primary CTA color

### Post-Form Trust Elements

- Privacy assurance: "We'll never share your email"
- Link to terms/privacy (present but not prominent)
- "Already have an account? Log in" — don't make returning users hunt for this

## Confirmation Screen

After signup, the confirmation screen should:
- Confirm what just happened ("Your account is ready")
- Set expectations ("Check your email to verify")
- Drive toward the aha moment immediately ("Start with [first action]")

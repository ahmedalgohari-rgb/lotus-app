# Lead Magnets

Create compelling lead magnet concepts and content for: **$ARGUMENTS**

## What This Skill Does

Design lead magnets that attract the right audience — not everyone with an email address, but the specific people who are likely to become customers.

## Lead Magnet Criteria

A great lead magnet:
- Solves one specific problem immediately (not eventually)
- Is consumed in under 20 minutes (respects the reader's time)
- Delivers a result, not just information
- Naturally bridges to the paid product
- Attracts buyers, not just browsers

## Lead Magnet Formats by Conversion Quality

Ranked from highest to lowest conversion quality (not traffic volume):

**Highest intent (captures buyers)**
- Free tool or calculator
- Email course (5-7 day sequence)
- Template + swipe file
- Mini-course or workshop replay

**Mid intent**
- Checklist or framework PDF
- Research report or benchmark data
- Webinar or live training
- Resource library

**Lower intent (broad, low commitment)**
- eBook or guide
- Newsletter subscription
- Podcast / YouTube series

## Process

### 1. Identify the Entry Point Problem

What is the problem the target audience is trying to solve right now — before they know they need the paid product?

For $ARGUMENTS, identify:
- The immediate problem (search-query level)
- The underlying goal behind it
- The gap that only the paid product fills permanently

### 2. Design the Lead Magnet

Choose a format and specify:
- **Title** — include the specific outcome ("The [X]-Minute [Result] [Framework/Template/Checklist]")
- **Format** — what type of content
- **Core promise** — what they get in 1 sentence
- **Delivery mechanism** — PDF, email series, tool, video
- **Length / scope** — how much content is right

### 3. Outline the Content

For the top 2 lead magnet ideas, provide:
- Complete section outline
- First section written in full
- Lead capture page headline and subhead

### 4. The Bridge

How does this lead magnet connect to the paid offer?
- What does the lead magnet help them accomplish?
- What's the next problem it reveals — the one the paid product solves?
- Where does the upgrade CTA appear?

## Output

Generate 5 lead magnet concepts with titles, core promises, and format. Develop the top pick into a full outline with bridge strategy and landing page copy.

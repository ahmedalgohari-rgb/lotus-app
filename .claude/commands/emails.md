# Emails

Write email copy for newsletters, drip sequences, and transactional messages: **$ARGUMENTS**

## What This Skill Does

Write emails people actually open, read, and act on — from onboarding sequences to newsletters to transactional notifications.

## Email Types and Approach

### Transactional Emails (welcome, receipt, reset, notification)

- **Clarity above everything** — the reader just did something and needs confirmation
- State what happened, what comes next, and what to do if something's wrong
- Inject one small moment of brand personality (it's the most-read email you send)
- Subject: descriptive, not clever

### Onboarding Drip (Day 0-30)

Day 0 — Welcome: Deliver on the promise, set expectations for what's coming
Day 1 — Quick win: Help them achieve something small in under 5 minutes
Day 3 — Social proof: Show them who else uses it and what they achieved
Day 7 — Feature spotlight: The one feature that most improves retention
Day 14 — Check-in: "Getting the most out of it?" + low-friction support offer
Day 30 — Investment: Show them what they've built / accomplished so far

### Newsletters

Structure for high-engagement newsletters:
- **Subject**: specific over clever; curiosity is fine if you deliver on it
- **Preview text**: extends the subject, adds a second hook
- **Opening line**: no "Hi [name], hope you're well" — get to the point immediately
- **One main thing**: longer newsletters that try to do 5 things do zero well
- **CTA**: one ask, placed where the reader is engaged (after the value, not before)

### Re-engagement / Win-Back

- Acknowledge the silence, don't pretend it didn't happen
- Lead with something new they may have missed
- Make the path back frictionless (one-click)
- Give permission to unsubscribe gracefully (it protects deliverability)

## Format Requirements

For each email provide:
- **Subject line** (with 2 alternates)
- **Preview text** (40-90 chars)
- **Body copy** (complete, ready to send)
- **CTA** (button text + placement note)
- **From name recommendation** (person vs. brand)

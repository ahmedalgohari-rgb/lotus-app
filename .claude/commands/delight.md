# Delight

Identify ways to surprise and delight customers for: **$ARGUMENTS**

## What This Skill Does

Find the moments where the product or brand can do more than expected — creating memories, loyalty, and word-of-mouth through unexpected positivity.

## The Delight Framework

Delight = Unexpected + Positive + Personal

All three must be present. Expected kindness is just good service. Unexpectedness without positivity is a shock. Neither lands without a personal feel.

## Process

### 1. Map the Customer Journey

List every touchpoint from awareness to renewal:
- Discovery / first impression
- Signup / onboarding
- First value moment ("aha" moment)
- Ongoing use / habit loop
- Reaching a milestone
- Encountering a problem / support
- Renewal / upgrade decision
- Advocacy / referral

### 2. Identify Delight Opportunities

For each touchpoint, ask:
- What does the customer expect here?
- What would be 10% better than expected?
- What would be genuinely surprising?
- Where does the experience currently feel robotic, generic, or cold?

### 3. Delight Tactics by Category

**Micro-delights** (small, scalable, in-product)
- Copy surprises: onboarding confirmation messages, empty states, error messages with personality
- Progress celebrations: confetti, milestone badges, streak acknowledgments
- Unexpected speed: pages that load faster than expected

**Personal moments** (higher effort, higher impact)
- Handwritten or personalized milestone notes for power users
- Proactive "we noticed you might need X" before they ask
- Remembering preferences without being asked

**Community and recognition**
- Featuring customers publicly (with permission)
- Early access to new features for engaged users
- Responding to social mentions with genuine specificity

**Recovery delight** (when things go wrong)
- Proactive outreach before the customer notices an issue
- Compensation that exceeds the inconvenience
- Personal follow-up after a resolved support issue

### 4. Prioritize by Impact and Effort

Rank each idea:
- Scalable at any volume vs. high-touch (requires manual work)
- One-time vs. repeatable
- Cost to implement

## Output

Generate 10 specific delight opportunities for the context in $ARGUMENTS — 5 low-effort/scalable, 5 higher-effort/high-impact. Include specific copy or experience design for each.

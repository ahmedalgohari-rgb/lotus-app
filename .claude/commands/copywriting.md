# Copywriting

Write high-converting marketing copy for: **$ARGUMENTS**

## What This Skill Does

Write copy that moves people — from awareness to desire to action. Grounded in the product, the audience, and a specific psychological mechanism.

## Before Writing

Establish these inputs from $ARGUMENTS or ask:
- **Product/offer**: What exactly is being sold or promoted?
- **Audience**: Who is reading this? What do they want and fear?
- **Stage**: Awareness / Consideration / Decision / Retention?
- **Format**: Headline, landing page, email, ad, product description?
- **Tone**: Brand voice guidance or adjectives

## Core Copywriting Frameworks

### PAS (Problem → Agitate → Solution)
Best for: ads, email subject lines, landing page heroes
- **Problem**: Name the specific pain in their words
- **Agitate**: Make it vivid — what's the cost of this problem?
- **Solution**: Introduce the product as the obvious answer

### AIDA (Attention → Interest → Desire → Action)
Best for: long-form sales pages, email sequences
- **Attention**: Headline that interrupts the scroll
- **Interest**: Build relevance fast — "This is for you if..."
- **Desire**: Benefits > features, social proof, risk reversal
- **Action**: One clear CTA, remove friction

### FAB (Feature → Advantage → Benefit)
Best for: product descriptions, feature callouts
- **Feature**: What it does
- **Advantage**: Why that matters
- **Benefit**: What the reader gains (emotional or practical)

## Copy Elements to Deliver

- **Headline** (3 variants minimum — curiosity, benefit, direct)
- **Subheadline** (expands the headline, qualifies the reader)
- **Body copy** (main argument, structured for scanners)
- **Social proof** (where to place it and suggested format)
- **CTA** (button text + surrounding copy)
- **Objection handling** (anticipate and address the top 2-3 objections)

## Output

Write the complete copy in full, then provide 2 headline alternatives and a brief rationale for the strategic choices made.

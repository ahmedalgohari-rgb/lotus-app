# Product Marketing

Develop product marketing positioning and messaging for: **$ARGUMENTS**

## What This Skill Does

Build the messaging architecture that makes a product's value undeniable — positioning, narrative, and the specific copy that brings it to life across channels.

## Product Marketing Framework

### 1. Positioning Statement

The internal document that everything else derives from. Not for public consumption — for alignment.

Format (Geoffrey Moore):
> For [target customer] who [has this need or problem], [product name] is a [category] that [key benefit]. Unlike [alternatives], [product] [key differentiator].

Write 3 variants and select the sharpest.

### 2. Audience Segmentation

Define the ICP (Ideal Customer Profile):
- Who they are (role, company size, industry)
- What they're trying to accomplish (job-to-be-done)
- What they've tried before and why it failed
- What they measure success by
- Their specific language for the problem (use their exact words in copy)

### 3. Messaging Hierarchy

**Primary message** — the single most important thing to communicate (one sentence)
**Supporting messages** — 3-5 claims that substantiate the primary message
**Proof points** — specific evidence for each supporting message (metrics, case studies, features)

### 4. Competitive Positioning

How does this product win against each major alternative?
- Name the comparison explicitly when useful
- Own the category or reframe the category entirely
- Build the narrative: "If you're using [X], you're solving the wrong problem"

### 5. Narrative Arc

The overarching story:
- **Before**: the world without this product (the pain, the workaround, the cost)
- **After**: the world with it (the transformation, the outcome)
- **Bridge**: why this product is the right path from before to after

### 6. Channel Translation

Adapt the core message for:
- **Website hero** — most concise version, clearest outcome
- **Ads** — hook-first, problem-aware, CTA-driven
- **Sales deck** — narrative arc with proof points
- **Email** — personal, conversational, one idea per send
- **Social** — platform-native format, share-worthy angle

## Output

Deliver: positioning statement, messaging hierarchy with proof points, competitive positioning for top 2 alternatives, and hero copy (headline + subhead + CTA) for the website.

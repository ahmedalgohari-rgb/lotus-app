# Shape

Direct shape, form, and visual composition for design elements: **$ARGUMENTS**

## What This Skill Does

Make deliberate shape and form decisions — from geometric choices to organic forms, from border radii to compositional silhouettes — with the visual logic to justify each choice.

## Shape as Communication

Every shape communicates:
- **Circles and curves** — friendly, complete, continuous, safe, approachable
- **Squares and rectangles** — stable, reliable, orderly, corporate, trustworthy
- **Triangles** — dynamic, directional, tension, risk, efficiency
- **Organic / blob shapes** — natural, human, creative, unpredictable
- **Geometric precision** — technical, systematic, engineered
- **Asymmetry** — creative, unique, modern, intentional disruption

The shape language should match the product's personality — not be chosen for aesthetics alone.

## Shape Decisions in UI

### Border Radius

This single decision dramatically shapes brand perception:
- **0px** (no radius) — brutalist, technical, raw, industrial
- **2-4px** — professional, subtle softness, enterprise
- **8px** — friendly, approachable, modern SaaS standard
- **12-16px** — consumer-friendly, mobile-native feel
- **24px+** — playful, rounded, strongly consumer
- **50% (pill)** — used for tags, badges, buttons when going fully rounded

Rule: pick one radius and apply it consistently. Use 2x the base radius for larger components.

### Composition and Silhouette

The overall shape of a layout or component:
- What is the dominant shape? (horizontal band, vertical column, centered block)
- Does it feel heavy or light? (more mass at bottom = stable, more mass at top = dynamic)
- Are the boundaries hard or soft? (full bleed vs. contained vs. floating)

### Negative Space as Shape

The space between elements is itself a shape.
- Generous negative space: premium, confident, relaxed
- Tight negative space: dense, information-rich, productive tool feel
- Shape the negative space deliberately — it frames what matters

### Organic vs. Geometric

- Full geometric: systematic, trustworthy, scalable (Stripe, Linear)
- Organic accents: human warmth within a structured system (Notion)
- Fully organic: expressive, creative-industry brands

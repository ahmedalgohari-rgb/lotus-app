# Image

## What This Skill Does

Write precise, effective prompts for AI image generators (Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion, Firefly) and give visual content direction for photographers and illustrators.

## Image Prompt Anatomy

A strong image prompt has these components in order:

1. **Subject** — who or what is the main focus
2. **Action/State** — what they're doing or how they appear
3. **Environment** — setting, background, context
4. **Lighting** — the most important technical element (determines mood)
5. **Style** — photographic, illustrative, painterly, graphic
6. **Mood** — the emotional register
7. **Technical specs** — aspect ratio, quality modifiers

## Lighting Vocabulary

Master these lighting descriptors for precise results:
- **Golden hour** — warm, low, directional, long shadows
- **Overcast** — soft, diffused, no shadows, neutral
- **Rim light** — backlit, subject silhouette, dramatic
- **Rembrandt** — side-lit, dramatic shadow triangle on face
- **Studio softbox** — clean, commercial, controlled
- **Candlelight / practical light** — warm, intimate, flickery
- **Neon** — colored, ambient, urban night

## Style Modifiers by Output Type

**Photography**: `DSLR, 35mm lens, f/1.8, shallow depth of field, editorial photography, shot for [magazine name]`

**Illustration**: `flat design vector illustration, editorial illustration, line art, [art director name]'s style`

**Product/Marketing**: `clean white background, product photography, commercial lighting, e-commerce`

**Brand/Identity**: `logo design, minimal, geometric, scalable, single color`

## Prompt Templates

### For product lifestyle:
`[Product] being used by [person description] in [environment], [lighting], [mood], [style], [aspect ratio]`

### For brand imagery:
`[Scene description], [color palette], [art direction style], editorial photography, [magazine reference]`

### For illustrations:
`[Subject and action], [style reference], flat design, [color palette], minimal background, vector illustration`

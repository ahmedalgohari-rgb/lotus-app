# Community Marketing

Build community marketing strategies and engagement playbooks for: **$ARGUMENTS**

## What This Skill Does

Design a community marketing strategy that creates genuine belonging, drives organic growth, and turns members into advocates — not just an audience.

## Process

### 1. Define Community Purpose

Answer before building anything:
- What shared identity or goal unites the members?
- What do members get from this community that they can't get elsewhere?
- What does the brand get? (feedback, content, referrals, retention)
- Hosted (owned forum/Discord) or parasocial (social media audience)? Both?

### 2. Platform Selection

Match platform to audience behavior:
- **Discord** — real-time, younger audience, gaming/dev/creator adjacent
- **Slack** — B2B, professional, existing team habits
- **Circle / Mighty Networks** — paid or exclusive communities, courses
- **Reddit** — anonymous, high-trust peer advice, existing subreddits
- **Facebook Groups** — older demographic, local/interest communities
- **LinkedIn** — B2B, professional development, thought leadership
- **Owned forum** — SEO value, full control, highest friction to join

### 3. Community Structure

Design the channel/category architecture:
- Onboarding channel (introduce yourself, rules, how to get value)
- Core topic channels (3-5 max to start)
- Help/support channel
- Off-topic / social channel
- Announcements (low volume, high signal)

### 4. Content and Engagement Cadence

Weekly rhythm for community health:
- **Daily**: Respond to questions within 24 hours
- **Weekly**: One discussion prompt or community challenge
- **Monthly**: AMA, guest expert, or member spotlight
- **Quarterly**: Community survey, celebrate milestones

### 5. Growth Loops

Tactics to grow the community:
- Invite-only early access (scarcity + exclusivity)
- Content seeded outside community links back in
- Member achievements shared publicly
- UGC and member spotlights on brand channels

## Output

Deliver: platform recommendation with rationale, channel structure, 30-day launch plan, weekly content calendar template, and 5 community growth tactics.

# Competitor Profiling

Conduct deep competitor analysis and profiling for: **$ARGUMENTS**

## What This Skill Does

Build a complete intelligence profile on a competitor — positioning, product, pricing, messaging, strengths, and exploitable weaknesses.

## Process

### 1. Identity and Positioning

- Full legal name, founding year, HQ, funding stage and total raised
- Their stated mission and tagline
- Who they say they're for (their ideal customer profile from their own website)
- Their positioning: what category do they claim to own?

### 2. Product Analysis

- Core features and how they work
- Where the product excels (based on positive reviews)
- Where the product fails (based on negative reviews — this is gold)
- Integrations and ecosystem
- Mobile vs. desktop vs. API capabilities
- Free tier limits, trial length

### 3. Pricing and Packaging

- Pricing tiers (names, prices, included features)
- What triggers an upgrade from free to paid?
- Annual vs. monthly discount
- Enterprise pricing approach (opaque vs. public)
- Where customers complain about pricing

### 4. Marketing and Messaging

- Primary marketing channels (SEO, paid ads, social, influencer, PLG)
- Top-performing content themes (based on what they publish most)
- Their narrative: what fear, aspiration, or identity do they sell to?
- Tone of voice: formal, playful, technical, emotional?
- What social proof they use most (logos, numbers, testimonials)

### 5. Weaknesses and Attack Vectors

Identify 3-5 specific exploitable gaps:
- Features customers request but they haven't built
- Segments they serve poorly (look at negative reviews)
- Pricing gaps where they're over- or under-charging
- Messaging blind spots (what they never talk about that customers care about)

## Output Format

```
COMPETITOR: [Name]

SNAPSHOT
[3-sentence summary of who they are and their position]

PRODUCT STRENGTHS
1. ...
2. ...

PRODUCT WEAKNESSES (from reviews)
1. ...
2. ...

[Tier names and prices]

OUR ATTACK VECTORS
1. [Gap] → [How we exploit it]
2. ...
```

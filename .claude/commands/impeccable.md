# Impeccable

Polish content to a publication-ready, impeccable standard for: **$ARGUMENTS**

## What This Skill Does

Take good content and make it flawless — the level of quality that makes readers trust every word because they sense the care behind every sentence.

## The Impeccable Standard

Publication-ready means:
- A copy editor at a major publication would approve it without changes
- Every sentence earns its place
- The reader never trips on phrasing, logic gaps, or inconsistency
- Formatting is perfectly consistent throughout
- Nothing is technically incorrect, ambiguous, or misleading

## Five-Pass Review

### Pass 1 — Logic and Structure

- Does the argument hold? Are there leaps in reasoning?
- Is the sequence correct? Does each paragraph follow from the previous?
- Is anything missing that a reader would need to understand the full picture?
- Is there anything included that doesn't belong?

### Pass 2 — Clarity and Precision

- Every sentence: is there a cleaner way to say this?
- Every claim: is it as specific as it could be?
- Every modifier: does it add information or just length?
- Ambiguous pronouns: does "it" / "they" / "this" clearly refer to the right thing?

### Pass 3 — Consistency

- Terminology: is the same thing called the same name throughout?
- Voice: does tone stay consistent or shift unexpectedly?
- Formatting: are headers, bullets, capitalization, and punctuation consistent?
- Numbers: spelled out vs. numerals — consistent rule applied?

### Pass 4 — Rhythm and Flow

- Read it aloud (or simulate doing so). Where does it stumble?
- Are sentences varied in length? (monotonous rhythm = unengaging)
- Do paragraph lengths vary? (visual rhythm matters)
- Does the ending land? Does the piece close, or just stop?

### Pass 5 — Proofreading

- Spelling, grammar, punctuation
- Proper nouns spelled correctly (brand names, people names, places)
- Consistent serial comma usage (Oxford or not — pick one)
- No orphaned words at end of lines in formatted output (where possible)

## Output

Deliver the final, impeccable version. Then provide a brief editorial note: what was changed at each pass and why. Note any judgment calls made (so the author can override if needed).

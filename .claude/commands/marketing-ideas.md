# Marketing Ideas

Generate creative marketing campaign ideas for: **$ARGUMENTS**

## What This Skill Does

Produce a range of marketing campaign concepts — from low-budget high-creativity plays to bigger bets — each with a clear mechanic, channel, and expected outcome.

## Idea Generation Framework

For each idea, the concept must answer:
- **The hook**: Why would someone care? What makes it worth attention?
- **The mechanic**: Exactly how does it work, step by step?
- **The channel**: Where does this live and spread?
- **The ask**: What action do we want the audience to take?
- **Why it fits**: Why is this right for the product, audience, and moment?

## Campaign Archetypes

Generate ideas across these archetypes:

**Organic / Content**
- Data report or research (own a statistic the industry shares)
- Contrarian opinion piece (disagree with conventional wisdom your audience holds)
- Comparison content (honest feature comparison that converts fence-sitters)
- Behind the scenes (how the product is built, customer stories, founder journey)

**Viral / Shareable**
- Free tool that solves a problem in 60 seconds
- Interactive quiz or assessment with personalized results
- Community challenge with UGC component
- Unexpected collaboration that creates press

**Community / Partnership**
- AMA in a relevant community
- Newsletter ad swap with complementary product
- Joint webinar or event with an audience you want

**Paid / Performance**
- Retargeting sequence (3-touch: awareness → proof → offer)
- Problem-aware search campaign (targeting symptom keywords, not product keywords)
- Lookalike campaign seeded from best customers

## Output Format

Generate 10 campaign ideas for $ARGUMENTS. For each:

```
IDEA: [Name]
Hook: [What makes it compelling]
Mechanic: [How it works]
Channel: [Where it runs]
Ask: [What action is wanted]
Effort: Low / Medium / High
Expected outcome: [Specific result]
```

Then develop the top 2 ideas into full briefs with timeline, copy direction, and success metrics.

# Launch

Build a product launch strategy and go-to-market plan for: **$ARGUMENTS**

## What This Skill Does

Create a complete launch plan — from pre-launch build-up through launch day execution to post-launch momentum — with specific tasks, copy, and timing.

## Launch Framework

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (4-6 weeks before)

**Build the Audience**
- Start a waitlist with email capture
- Define the launch angle (what's the story? why now? who cares?)
- Identify launch communities: Product Hunt, Hacker News, relevant subreddits, newsletters
- Recruit 10-20 beta users for testimonials and feedback
- Prepare social proof: screenshots, numbers, quotes

**Create Launch Assets**
- Demo video (60-90 seconds maximum, show the product working)
- Launch landing page with waitlist CTA
- Email sequence for waitlist (3-email drip: teaser → value → launch day)
- Press kit: logo, screenshots, key stats, founder bio

**Seed the Community**
- Post in communities where your audience lives (value-first, not promotional)
- Identify 5 people who will upvote/share on launch day
- Warm up your email list with relevant content (not just the launch announcement)

### Phase 2 — Launch Week

**Day 0 (midnight) — Go Live**
- Product goes public
- Email waitlist at 6am their time
- Schedule social posts across all channels

**Day 1 — Product Hunt**
- Post at 12:01am PST
- Comment actively all day
- Ask your network to upvote (with the direct link, not just "check out my launch")
- Reply to every comment on the PH listing

**Day 2-3 — Media and Communities**
- Post in relevant subreddits and forums (tailored, not copy-paste)
- Email journalists/newsletter writers with the launch story
- LinkedIn post with the "why I built this" angle

**Day 4-7 — Convert and Learn**
- Email non-converted waitlist with objection-handling copy
- Publish a launch recap post (metrics, learnings, gratitude — these perform well)
- Interview your first 10 customers

### Phase 3 — Post-Launch (Week 2+)

- Analyze: where did signups come from?
- Double down on top channel
- Address top onboarding drop-off in product
- Collect testimonials from happy early users

## Output

Deliver: full launch timeline with specific tasks per day, copy for launch email, Product Hunt description, and social posts for launch day.

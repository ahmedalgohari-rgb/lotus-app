# Customer Research

Develop customer personas, research plans, and insights for: **$ARGUMENTS**

## What This Skill Does

Synthesize or generate customer intelligence — from survey design to persona creation to insight extraction — so product and marketing decisions are grounded in reality.

## Process

### 1. Research Goals

Define what decision this research should inform:
- "We need to understand why users churn in week 2"
- "We want to find the right positioning for a new feature"
- "We need to validate demand before building X"

Research without a decision to inform is just data collection.

### 2. Research Methods (choose based on goal)

**Qualitative — Why and How**
- User interviews (30-45 min, open-ended, 5-8 participants per segment)
- Usability testing (observe real task completion)
- Jobs-to-be-done interviews (timeline interview method)
- Support ticket analysis (pattern mine for pain points)

**Quantitative — How Many and How Much**
- In-app surveys (NPS, CES, PMF)
- Exit surveys (on cancellation or exit intent)
- Cohort analysis (behavioral data)
- Keyword research (proxy for unsatisfied demand)

### 3. Interview Guide

For user interviews, generate a question guide:
- Opening: context-setting (current workflow, role, tools)
- Core: job-to-be-done exploration
- Emotional: frustrations, aspirations, workarounds
- Behavioral: "tell me about the last time you..."
- Closing: ranking, priorities, willingness to pay signal

### 4. Persona Building

Structure each persona with:
- **Name and role** (archetype, not demographic)
- **Job-to-be-done** — the functional, emotional, and social jobs
- **Current workflow** — how they solve the problem today
- **Frustrations with current solution** — the pull toward something better
- **Success metrics** — how they measure if something is working
- **Buying triggers** — what causes them to evaluate new solutions
- **Objections** — what holds them back from switching

### 5. Insight Synthesis

From research data, extract:
- The top 3 unmet needs (stated in the customer's words)
- The biggest gap between expectation and reality
- The language customers use (feed directly to copywriting)
- Surprising findings that challenge current assumptions

## Output

Deliver research plan or persona document based on $ARGUMENTS. If analyzing existing data, produce an insight report with direct quotes and strategic implications.

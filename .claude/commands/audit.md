# Audit

Audit content, strategy, or UX and provide prioritized recommendations for: **$ARGUMENTS**

## What This Skill Does

Conduct a structured audit that surfaces what's working, what's broken, and what's missing — then ranks fixes by impact vs. effort.

## Process

### 1. Establish Audit Scope

- What is being audited? (landing page, onboarding flow, email sequence, content library, pricing page, UX flow)
- What is the goal? (conversions, retention, clarity, SEO, brand consistency)
- What does "good" look like here? (benchmark or best-practice standard)

### 2. Evaluate Against Criteria

Score each element 1-5 across relevant dimensions:

**For Content**: Clarity, accuracy, tone consistency, CTA strength, keyword presence
**For UX/UI**: Discoverability, friction, visual hierarchy, error handling, mobile behavior
**For Strategy**: Audience fit, channel alignment, funnel coverage, differentiation
**For SEO**: Title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure, internal linking, page speed signals

### 3. Classify Findings

- **Critical** — actively hurting performance, fix immediately
- **High** — significant untapped opportunity
- **Medium** — incremental improvement worth scheduling
- **Low** — polish items, tackle last

### 4. Impact vs. Effort Matrix

Plot every finding:
- Quick wins (high impact, low effort) — do first
- Major projects (high impact, high effort) — plan and resource
- Fill-ins (low impact, low effort) — batch and ship
- Deprioritize (low impact, high effort) — skip or defer

## Output Format

```
AUDIT SUMMARY
Scope: [what was audited]
Overall score: [X/10]
Top strength: [one sentence]
Biggest gap: [one sentence]

FINDINGS
[Critical] [Item]: [Problem] → [Fix]
[High]     [Item]: [Problem] → [Fix]
[Medium]   [Item]: [Problem] → [Fix]

QUICK WINS (do this week)
1. [Action] — Expected outcome: [result]
2. ...

ROADMAP
Phase 1 (this sprint): ...
Phase 2 (next month): ...
```

# Popups

## What This Skill Does

Write popups that convert without annoying — by getting the timing, trigger, value proposition, and copy right.

## Popup Philosophy

Popups fail when they interrupt. They succeed when they intercept — appearing at exactly the moment the user needs what the popup offers.

The test: would the user thank you for this popup, or would they close it without reading?

## Popup Types and When to Use Each

**Exit-intent popup**
- Trigger: cursor moves toward browser chrome
- Use for: last chance offer, newsletter signup, feedback
- Do NOT use for: first-time visitors within 30 seconds of landing

**Scroll-triggered popup**
- Trigger: 50-70% scroll depth (they're engaged)
- Use for: content upgrades, related offers, newsletter
- Best for: blog posts and long-form content

**Time-delay popup**
- Trigger: 30-60 seconds on page (they're reading, not bouncing)
- Use for: lead magnets, trial offers
- Avoid: under 15 seconds (too aggressive)

**Click-triggered popup**
- Trigger: user clicks something that implies readiness
- Use for: pricing details, demo requests, feature deep-dives
- This is the least intrusive popup (user opted in)

**Behavioral trigger**
- Trigger: user completes an action (reaches step 3, adds to cart, etc.)
- Use for: upsell, cross-sell, completion encouragement

## Copy Framework

### Headline
- State what they get, not what you want
- "Get the free [outcome] template" not "Subscribe to our newsletter"
- Under 8 words

### Body (optional, 1-2 sentences max)
- Specific proof or reassurance
- Who it's for + what they'll be able to do

### CTA Button
- Specificity: "Send me the template" beats "Submit"
- First person: "Start my free trial" beats "Start free trial"
- Length: 2-5 words

### Dismiss text
- Honest, not manipulative: "No thanks" not "No, I hate saving money"
- Dark patterns on dismiss text destroy brand trust

## Design Requirements

- Mobile: full-screen or bottom sheet only (small popups on mobile = untappable X buttons)
- Timing: never on first pageview for first-time visitors
- Frequency cap: once per session maximum, once per week maximum
- Close button: always visible, always at least 44x44px touch target

# Ad Creative

Write ad creative copy and briefs for: **$ARGUMENTS**

## What This Skill Does

Produce ready-to-use ad creative — headlines, body copy, CTAs, and creative briefs — for paid channels.

## Process

### 1. Clarify Context (if not provided)

- Product/service and core value prop
- Target audience (demographics, pain points, awareness level)
- Channel (Meta, Google, TikTok, LinkedIn, Display, OOH)
- Objective (awareness, clicks, installs, purchases, leads)
- Tone (playful, urgent, authoritative, emotional)

### 2. Write Multiple Variants

For each ad, produce:

**Headline** (max 30 chars for Google, 40 for Meta)
- Lead with the benefit, not the feature
- Use numbers when possible ("Save 3 hours/week")
- Test one emotional angle and one rational angle

**Body Copy** (varies by channel)
- Hook in line 1 — speak to the pain or desire
- Bridge to the solution in 1-2 sentences
- Social proof where relevant (users, ratings, results)
- CTA that matches the offer exactly

**CTA Button** (3-5 words)
- Action-oriented: "Start Free Trial", "Get My Quote", "Download Now"

### 3. Creative Brief (for design handoff)

- Visual direction: what should the image/video show?
- Color and mood guidance
- Text overlay suggestions
- Any legal/compliance flags

## Output Format

Produce **3 headline variants** and **2 body copy variants** minimum.
Label each with the angle it tests (e.g., [Fear of missing out], [Social proof], [Direct benefit]).
End with a one-paragraph creative brief for the design team.

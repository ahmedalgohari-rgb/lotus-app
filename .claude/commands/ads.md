# Ads

## What This Skill Does

Produce polished, channel-native ad copy for Google Search, Meta (Facebook/Instagram), LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, and display networks.

## Channel Specs & Approach

### Google Search Ads
- 3 headlines (max 30 chars each), 2 descriptions (max 90 chars each)
- Lead with the keyword, promise the outcome, include a differentiator
- Use ad extensions: sitelinks, callouts, structured snippets

### Meta (Facebook/Instagram)
- Primary text: 125 chars before truncation — hook must be in first line
- Headline: 27 chars
- Description: 27 chars
- Write for thumb-stopping: lead with a tension, surprise, or bold claim
- Separate copy for feed vs. Stories vs. Reels

### LinkedIn Ads
- Professional tone, but not boring
- Speak to career outcomes, business results, or professional identity
- Sponsored Content: 150 char intro, 70 char headline
- Message Ads: conversational, specific, low-friction CTA

### TikTok / Short Video Scripts
- Hook in first 3 seconds (say something unexpected or ask a question)
- 15–30 second structure: Hook → Problem → Solution → CTA
- Write as spoken word — casual, energetic, direct

### YouTube Pre-Roll (15–30s)
- Earn attention before the skip button: make them curious or laugh in 5s
- Problem agitation → solution → proof → CTA

## Process

1. Identify channel(s) and objective from $ARGUMENTS
2. Write platform-native copy in correct format
3. Provide 2-3 variants per placement
4. Note the angle each variant tests
5. Flag any compliance or policy considerations

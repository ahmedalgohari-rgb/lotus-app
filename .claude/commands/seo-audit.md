# SEO Audit

Conduct a comprehensive SEO audit with prioritized recommendations for: **$ARGUMENTS**

## What This Skill Does

Systematically evaluate SEO health across technical, on-page, and off-page dimensions — then prioritize fixes by impact on organic visibility and traffic.

## Audit Dimensions

### 1. Technical SEO

**Crawlability**
- robots.txt: is anything important blocked?
- XML sitemap: exists, submitted to Search Console, no 404s or redirects in sitemap
- Canonical tags: self-referential canonicals on all pages, no conflicting canonicals
- URL structure: clean, descriptive, lowercase, hyphens not underscores

**Indexation**
- Noindex audit: any important pages accidentally noindexed?
- Duplicate content: same content on multiple URLs (pagination, parameters, www vs. non-www)
- Thin content: pages under 300 words, low word count to ad ratio

**Page Experience**
- Core Web Vitals: LCP < 2.5s, CLS < 0.1, INP < 200ms
- Mobile-friendly: responsive design, no horizontal scroll, tap targets ≥ 44px
- HTTPS: all pages, no mixed content warnings

**Structured Data**
- Schema markup present and valid
- No errors in Google Rich Results Test

### 2. On-Page SEO

**Title Tags**
- Unique per page
- Primary keyword in first 60 characters
- No keyword stuffing
- Brand name at end: "Page Topic | Brand Name"

**Meta Descriptions**
- Unique per page (not auto-generated)
- 120-155 characters
- Includes primary keyword
- Compelling to drive CTR (this is ad copy, not just a summary)

**Heading Structure**
- One H1 per page
- H1 contains primary keyword
- Logical H2-H6 hierarchy (no skipping levels)
- Headers answer real user questions (featured snippet opportunity)

**Content Quality**
- Primary keyword in first 100 words
- Semantic keywords / entities included naturally
- Internal links to relevant pages (minimum 3 per article)
- External links to authoritative sources where relevant
- Images: alt text on all images, descriptive file names

### 3. Off-Page SEO

**Backlink Profile**
- Total referring domains (quantity)
- Quality of referring domains (DA/DR distribution)
- Anchor text diversity (no over-optimized anchor text)
- Toxic links: any spammy domains to disavow?

**Brand Signals**
- NAP consistency for local businesses
- Mentions without links (link reclamation opportunities)

## Priority Matrix

Rank all findings:
- **P0 Critical**: Blocking indexation or causing significant traffic loss
- **P1 High**: Clear ranking improvement available
- **P2 Medium**: Incremental improvement
- **P3 Low**: Best practices, do when convenient

## Output

Deliver: findings by dimension, priority matrix, top 10 fixes by expected impact, and a 30-day quick win list.

# AI SEO

AI-powered SEO strategy and content optimization for: **$ARGUMENTS**

## What This Skill Does

Develop SEO strategies that account for both traditional search and AI-powered discovery (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Claude).

## Process

### 1. Keyword Intelligence

- Identify **primary keyword** and cluster of 10-15 semantically related terms
- Categorize by intent: informational, navigational, commercial, transactional
- Flag **AI Overview trigger queries** — questions where Google shows AI-generated answers
- Identify **zero-click risk**: queries where users get answers without clicking
- Recommend **long-tail opportunities** with lower competition

### 2. Content Architecture

- Recommended **H1, H2, H3 structure** for the page/post
- Identify the **featured snippet format** to target (paragraph, list, table)
- Suggest **FAQ section** with exact-match question phrasing
- Recommend **word count range** based on SERP competitive analysis
- Entity coverage: people, places, concepts that should be mentioned

### 3. AI Visibility Optimization (AIO)

- Write content that answers questions **directly and crisply** (AI systems quote concise answers)
- Structure with **clear topic sentences** per paragraph
- Include **definitions** of key terms (AI systems love to quote these)
- Ensure **author/source authority signals** are present
- Add **structured data markup** recommendations

### 4. Technical SEO Checklist

- Title tag (under 60 chars, keyword near front)
- Meta description (under 155 chars, includes CTA)
- URL slug (short, keyword-rich, no stop words)
- Internal linking opportunities
- Page speed and Core Web Vitals flags

### 5. Output

Provide a complete brief: target keyword, content outline, title tag, meta description, and top 3 optimization priorities.

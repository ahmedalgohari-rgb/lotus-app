# Pricing

Design pricing strategy, page copy, and tier packaging for: **$ARGUMENTS**

## What This Skill Does

Build a pricing model that captures the right revenue, reduces friction, and communicates value — not just lists features.

## Pricing Strategy Framework

### 1. Pricing Model Selection

Choose the right model for the business:
- **Per seat** — B2B, team collaboration tools; scales with usage, aligns with value
- **Usage-based** — API, infrastructure, communication; low barrier, high ceiling
- **Flat fee** — simple, predictable; works when usage doesn't vary much
- **Freemium** — PLG growth driver; requires clear upgrade trigger
- **Hybrid** — base subscription + usage overages

For $ARGUMENTS, recommend and justify the best model.

### 2. Tier Design

Three tiers is the research-backed default (though two or four can work):
- **Free / Starter** — enough to get real value, enough to hit a natural limit
- **Pro / Growth** — the core paid tier (most revenue here)
- **Business / Enterprise** — for teams, advanced needs, security/compliance

For each tier, define:
- Who it's for (be specific: "solo freelancers" not "individuals")
- Core feature inclusions
- The one limit that drives upgrades
- Price point

### 3. Price Anchoring

Structure the page so the eye goes where you want:
- Most popular tier in the center or highlighted
- Annual pricing shown by default (or toggle, but pre-select annual)
- Decoy tier: make Pro look like exceptional value vs. Business
- Show the per-month-when-billed-annually price (not the annual total)

### 4. Pricing Page Copy

**Header**:
- Headline: emphasize simplicity or value, not just "Pricing"
- Subhead: "Start free. Upgrade when you're ready." or ROI framing

**Per tier**:
- Tier name: evocative, not generic ("Maker" not "Standard")
- Price: clean, no cents on round numbers
- Tagline: who it's for in 5 words
- Features: benefit-framed bullets (what you can do, not what's included)
- CTA: different text per tier ("Get started free" / "Start free trial" / "Contact sales")

**Below the table**:
- FAQ addressing top 3 pricing objections
- Enterprise CTA (custom pricing, dedicated support)
- Guarantee / risk reversal copy

### 5. Upgrade Triggers

What limits drive users from free → paid, paid → enterprise?
Design these deliberately, not as accidents.

## Output

Deliver: pricing model recommendation, tier structure with feature lists, full pricing page copy, and FAQ for top 3 objections.

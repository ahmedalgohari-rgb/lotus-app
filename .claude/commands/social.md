# Social

Create social media content across platforms for: **$ARGUMENTS**

## What This Skill Does

Write platform-native social content — not the same post copied across channels, but content that works with how each platform's algorithm, culture, and format actually function.

## Platform-by-Platform Guide

### LinkedIn

- **What performs**: personal stories with a business lesson, counterintuitive takes, specific tactical advice, vulnerable founder moments
- **Format**: short paragraphs (1-2 sentences), line breaks between every paragraph, no links in post body (comment them instead)
- **Opening line**: scroll-stopping without clickbait. Statement, not question. Specific number or surprising claim.
- **Length**: 150-300 words for most posts; long-form (700-1000 words) once content is proven
- **CTA**: ask a question to drive comments (algorithm reward)

### Twitter / X

- **What performs**: hot takes, precise observations, threads with payoff, replies to high-traffic conversations
- **Format for threads**: hook tweet (the payoff or claim) → numbered points → final tweet with CTA or summary
- **Hook rules**: under 100 chars, no em dash, implies payoff, specific over vague
- **Single tweet**: complete thought, no trailing "a thread" unless it's a thread

### Instagram

- **What performs**: visual storytelling, behind-the-scenes, before/after, carousels with real information value
- **Caption structure**: hook (first line visible before "more") → story or value → CTA → hashtags as last comment
- **Carousels**: each slide advances a story or teaches one thing. Slide 1 must be worth saving.
- **Hashtags**: 5-10 specific and relevant, not broad (100M posts = no reach)

### TikTok / Reels

- **Hook**: first 3 seconds determines everything — visual or verbal pattern interrupt
- **Format**: teach something in 30-60 seconds, show don't tell, native captions
- **Script structure**: hook → context → payoff → CTA
- **Trending audio**: use trending sounds only when naturally fitting

## Content Pillars for $ARGUMENTS

- Product / feature (what we built and why)

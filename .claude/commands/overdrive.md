# Overdrive

Amplify and supercharge content for maximum impact for: **$ARGUMENTS**

## What This Skill Does

Take content that's good and push it to 11 — amplifying energy, stakes, urgency, and memorability without losing credibility.

## Overdrive is NOT

- Hyperbole without backing ("THE GREATEST THING EVER")
- Exclamation points as a substitute for substance
- Buzzwords that sound big but mean nothing ("revolutionary," "disruptive," "game-changing")
- Louder version of mediocre content

## Overdrive IS

- Making the stakes vivid and real
- Writing like the reader's attention is about to leave (it is)
- Specificity so precise it shocks
- Rhythm that builds momentum
- The version of the idea that the author was too cautious to write

## Amplification Techniques

### Raise the Stakes

Weak: "This will save you time."
Overdrive: "You're losing 11 hours a week to this. That's 572 hours a year. That's 14 weeks of your life, every single year."

### Specificity Shock

Weak: "Thousands of companies use this."
Overdrive: "37,441 teams switched last quarter. That's 103 per day."

### Contrast and Contrast Again

Weak: "This is faster than alternatives."
Overdrive: "Competitors take 45 minutes. This takes 90 seconds. Not kidding."

### Short Sentences at the Peak

Build up, then cut. The shortest sentence in the passage should carry the most weight.

### The Direct Challenge

Address the reader's skepticism head-on at peak energy: "You've heard this before. You haven't heard this."

### End on Power

The last sentence should be the hardest to forget. Readers remember the ending more than the middle — write backwards from the ending if needed.

## Output

Rewrite $ARGUMENTS in overdrive mode. Show what changed. Flag anything pushed too far (where credibility may be strained) so the author can dial it back if needed.

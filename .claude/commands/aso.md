# ASO — App Store Optimization

Optimize App Store and Google Play presence for: **$ARGUMENTS**

## What This Skill Does

Write and optimize every ASO element to maximize visibility, click-through, and install conversion rates.

## Process

### 1. Keyword Research

- Identify **primary keyword** (highest volume, highest relevance)
- Build a list of **15-20 secondary keywords** covering use cases, features, competitors
- Classify each: high/medium/low competition
- Flag keywords where **top 10 apps are weak** (opportunity)
- Apple App Store: keywords field is 100 chars — every character counts, no spaces, use commas

### 2. Title (30 chars)

- Brand name + pipe or dash + primary keyword
- Example: "Lotus - Plant Care & Identify"
- The title carries the most algorithmic weight — keyword here ranks faster

### 3. Subtitle / Short Description (30 chars — Apple / 80 chars — Google)

- Secondary keyword + compelling hook
- Speaks to the core job-to-be-done in plain language

### 4. Full Description

**First 3 lines are critical** (visible before "more"):
- Lead with the strongest benefit
- Include primary keyword naturally
- End with a social proof signal or hook to expand

**Body structure**:
- Feature highlights in bullet format (easier to skim)
- Use ALL CAPS section headers (they render as visual breaks)
- Include keywords naturally — not stuffed
- End with a CTA ("Download free" or "Start your free trial")

### 5. Screenshots & Preview Video

Direction for each of the 3 most important screenshots:
- Screenshot 1: core value prop — what the app does in one glance
- Screenshot 2: most popular/impressive feature
- Screenshot 3: social proof, community, or results

Include suggested caption overlays (3-5 words, large text, benefit-focused).

### 6. Ratings & Reviews Strategy

- Prompt for reviews at peak happiness moments (after a win, not after a crash)
- Suggested in-app review trigger timing
- Response templates for 1-star reviews

## Output

Deliver: title, subtitle, keyword field, full description, screenshot direction, and a keyword priority list.

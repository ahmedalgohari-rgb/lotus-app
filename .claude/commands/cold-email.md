# Cold Email

Write cold email sequences that get replies for: **$ARGUMENTS**

## What This Skill Does

Write cold outreach emails that respect the recipient's time, lead with value, and make the ask irresistibly easy to say yes to.

## Cold Email Principles

- **One email = one idea** — never cram multiple asks or benefits
- **No flattery** — "I love your work" is noise, skip it
- **Relevance over reach** — one hyper-relevant line beats three generic ones
- **The ask must be tiny** — "15 minutes" or "a quick reply" not "a partnership call"
- **Subject lines**: short (3-6 words), curiosity or specificity, never clickbait

## Sequence Structure

### Email 1 — The Hook (Day 0)

- Line 1: One specific observation about them (shows you did homework)
- Line 2: What you do, for whom, and the outcome you deliver — in one sentence
- Line 3: The specific value you can offer them
- CTA: One low-friction ask (reply, link click, 10-minute call)
- Length: Under 100 words

### Email 2 — The Value Add (Day 3-4)

- Don't follow up — bring something new
- Attach a resource, insight, or case study relevant to them
- One sentence referencing Email 1
- Re-state the ask from Email 1
- Length: Under 80 words

### Email 3 — The Permission to Say No (Day 7-10)

- "I don't want to keep sending emails if this isn't relevant"
- Ask if timing is wrong or if there's a better contact
- Keep door open, maintain goodwill
- Length: Under 50 words

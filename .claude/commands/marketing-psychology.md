# Marketing Psychology

## What This Skill Does

Use established cognitive and behavioral psychology principles to make marketing more persuasive — not manipulative, but accurately calibrated to how people actually make decisions.

## Core Principles and Applications

### Cognitive Load
**Principle**: Working memory is limited. Complexity kills conversion.
**Application**: Reduce choices (paradox of choice), shorten forms, simplify pricing pages, one CTA per page.

### Loss Aversion (Kahneman/Tversky)
**Principle**: Losses feel ~2x more powerful than equivalent gains.
**Application**: Frame as what they lose by not acting, not what they gain. "Stop losing 3 hours a day" beats "Save 3 hours a day."

### Social Proof
**Principle**: People use others' behavior as a proxy for correct behavior.
**Application**: Show real numbers ("12,000 teams"), named testimonials with specific results, logos of recognizable customers, review scores with count.

### Anchoring
**Principle**: The first number seen influences all subsequent judgments.
**Application**: Show the higher price first (annual vs. monthly), show competitor pricing first, show the value before the cost.

### Scarcity and Urgency
**Principle**: Limited availability increases perceived value.
**Application**: Countdown timers only when real, limited seats/spots, cohort closes, waitlists. Fake scarcity destroys trust — use only when genuine.

### Reciprocity
**Principle**: People feel obligated to return favors.
**Application**: Give genuine value before asking (lead magnet, free tool, insightful content). The ask that follows feels natural, not extractive.

### The Endowment Effect
**Principle**: People overvalue things they already possess.
**Application**: Free trials (once they have it, loss aversion to losing it), data portability messaging, "your [data/progress/setup]" framing.

### Identity and Self-Concept
**Principle**: People act consistently with their identity.
**Application**: Frame the product as the tool that "people like you" use. "Join 50,000 engineers who…" sells to identity, not features.

### Processing Fluency
**Principle**: Easy-to-process messages are judged as more true and likeable.
**Application**: Shorter sentences, common words, clean visual design, familiar structure. Hard to read = hard to trust.

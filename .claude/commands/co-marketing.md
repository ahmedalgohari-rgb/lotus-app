# Co-Marketing

Design co-marketing partnership strategies and pitch materials for: **$ARGUMENTS**

## What This Skill Does

Identify the right partners, structure a mutually beneficial campaign, and write the pitch that gets a "yes."

## Process

### 1. Define the Partnership Opportunity

- What is the product/brand seeking a partner?
- What audience do you need to reach that you don't own yet?
- What do you offer in return? (audience, distribution, content, technology, credibility)
- What is the campaign goal? (email list growth, trial signups, brand awareness, content reach)

### 2. Identify Partner Criteria

Ideal co-marketing partner characteristics:
- **Audience overlap**: same buyer persona, non-competing product
- **Audience size parity**: roughly similar reach (or complementary if one has scale, one has depth)
- **Brand alignment**: similar values, quality perception, tone
- **Motivated**: they have a launch, growth goal, or audience gap you can fill

Generate a list of 10 specific partner candidates based on $ARGUMENTS.

### 3. Structure the Deal

Common co-marketing formats:
- **Co-authored content** (blog, report, webinar) — both promote
- **Bundle offer** — combined discount, promoted to both lists
- **Newsletter swap** — each mentions the other in one send
- **Joint webinar or event** — shared audience, split production
- **Product integration** — in-app cross-promotion

For each format, define: what each party contributes, what each party gets, timeline, and success metric.

### 4. Write the Pitch

One-page partnership pitch structure:
1. Why you're reaching out (specific, not generic)
2. Who your audience is and why it's valuable to them
3. The specific campaign idea with clear mutual benefit
4. What you're asking for and what you're offering
5. Easy next step (30-min call, not a contract)

## Output

Deliver: partner criteria, 10 candidate partners with rationale, 2-3 campaign format options, and a ready-to-send pitch email.

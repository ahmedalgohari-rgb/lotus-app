# Clarify

Simplify and clarify complex content or messaging for: **$ARGUMENTS**

## What This Skill Does

Take dense, jargon-heavy, or confusing content and rewrite it so the intended audience understands it immediately — without losing accuracy.

## Process

### 1. Identify the Audience

Before rewriting, establish:
- Who is reading this? (technical expertise, familiarity with the domain)
- What do they already know?
- What decision or action does this content support?
- What grade level is appropriate? (default: Grade 8 / Flesch-Kincaid 60+)

### 2. Find the Clarity Killers

Scan the content for:
- **Jargon** — technical terms with no plain-language equivalent provided
- **Abstraction** — claims with no concrete example or anchor
- **Buried lede** — the key point is in paragraph 4, not paragraph 1
- **Passive voice** — obscures who does what
- **Nominalization** — "make a decision" → "decide"; "provide assistance" → "help"
- **Long sentences** — anything over 25 words is a candidate for splitting
- **Acronyms without expansion** on first use
- **Hedging stacks** — "it is generally considered that" → just say the thing

### 3. Rewrite Principles

- One idea per sentence
- Lead with the conclusion, follow with the evidence
- Use second person ("you") to make it direct
- Replace abstract nouns with concrete actions
- Add an example for every non-obvious claim
- Cut anything that doesn't serve the reader's decision or action

### 4. Verify

After rewriting, confirm:
- A reader unfamiliar with the topic could explain the main point back
- No information was lost — only the fog was removed
- Tone matches the original intent (formal/casual, brand voice)

## Output Format

```
ORIGINAL
[original text]

CLARIFIED
[rewritten text]

CHANGES MADE
- [Specific change and why]
- ...

READING LEVEL
Before: [grade/score]
After:  [grade/score]
```

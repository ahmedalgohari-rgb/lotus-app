# Content Strategy

Plan content strategy and editorial calendars for: **$ARGUMENTS**

## What This Skill Does

Build a content strategy that serves business goals, not just fills a publishing schedule — with an editorial calendar ready to execute.

## Process

### 1. Define Content Goals

Map every piece of content to one of:
- **Attract** — bring new audiences in (SEO, social, PR)
- **Convert** — move prospects toward a decision (case studies, comparisons, demos)
- **Retain** — keep existing customers engaged and successful (tutorials, updates, community)
- **Establish authority** — build category credibility (research, opinions, data)

What percentage split between these goals is appropriate for $ARGUMENTS right now?

### 2. Audience and Topic Mapping

For each audience segment:
- What questions are they asking at each funnel stage?
- What formats do they consume? (video, long-form, newsletters, short social)
- What platforms do they live on?
- What search intent drives their queries?

Generate a topic cluster map:
- 3-5 pillar topics (broad, high-volume)
- 5-10 cluster topics per pillar (specific, long-tail)

### 3. Content Mix and Formats

Recommend a mix based on resources and goals:
- Long-form blog (1500-3000 words) — SEO, authority
- Short-form posts — social, newsletters
- Video / tutorial — activation, retention
- Data / research — PR, backlinks, authority
- Case studies — conversion
- Templates / tools — lead generation

### 4. Editorial Calendar

Build a 4-week calendar:

| Week | Title | Format | Goal | Channel | CTA |
|------|-------|--------|------|---------|-----|
| 1    | ...   | ...    | ...  | ...     | ... |

### 5. Distribution Plan

Content without distribution is wasted. For each piece:
- Primary channel (where it lives)
- Repurposing channels (where it's adapted and shared)
- Outreach list (who to share it with for amplification)

## Output

Deliver: goal mapping, topic cluster map, format recommendations, 4-week editorial calendar, and distribution checklist.

# Polish

Polish and refine content, copy, or design to a professional standard for: **$ARGUMENTS**

## What This Skill Does

Take something that's functionally complete and give it the final 20% of craft that separates amateur from professional work.

## What Polishing Addresses

Polishing is the layer between "done" and "great." It addresses:
- The rough edges that skilled readers will notice
- Inconsistencies that undermine credibility
- Missing micro-decisions that create a sense of care (or its absence)
- The feeling of something that was shipped vs. crafted

## Polish Checklist by Medium

### Written Content

**Consistency**
- Terminology: same thing, same word, throughout
- Capitalization: consistent for titles, headers, proper nouns
- Numbers: spelled out (one to nine) vs. numerals (10+), rule applied consistently
- Serial comma: chosen and applied throughout
- Em dashes, en dashes, hyphens: used correctly and consistently
- Quotation marks: straight vs. curly (typographer's quotes preferred in published work)

**Flow**
- Transitions between paragraphs feel natural
- Sentence length varies (not every sentence the same structure)
- No abrupt ending — the piece closes, not just stops
- Headers are parallel in structure if they're at the same level

**Final scan**
- Double spaces between sentences (remnant of typewriter era — remove)
- Repeated words within 2 sentences of each other
- Widows and orphans in formatted output (single words on last line)

### UI Copy

- Button text: consistent verb form ("Save" not "Save" and "Saving" interchangeably)
- Error messages: specific and helpful, not generic
- Empty states: written, not blank
- Placeholder text: informative, not "Type here..."
- Success messages: present, with specific confirmation ("Changes saved" not just a checkmark)

### Design Polish

- Alignment: everything on a grid, nothing floating
- Spacing: consistent scale applied, nothing eyeballed
- Typography: no rogue font weights or sizes outside the system
- Icons: single icon set, consistent size and stroke weight
- Color: no off-palette colors snuck in during iteration

# Lotus - Marketing Brief
## Plant Care App for Egypt | Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok

---

## 1. What Is Lotus?

Lotus is a bilingual (Arabic/English) plant care app built specifically for Egypt. It uses AI-powered plant identification, real-time Cairo weather data, and a curated database of 100+ plants to give personalized, seasonal care recommendations — in Egyptian Arabic.

**One-liner:** "Snap a photo, know your plant, keep it alive — in Egyptian Arabic."

**Tagline (EN):** Care for your plants at home. Grow with expert guidance.
**Tagline (AR):** نباتاتك في أمان معانا. رعاية سهلة ونصائح مخصصة.
*(Your plants are safe with us. Easy care and personalized tips.)*

**Available on:** iOS (App Store) — Bundle ID: `com.lotus.plantcare`

## 2. What Can Lotus Do?

### Core Features

| Feature | What It Does | Marketing Hook |
|---------|-------------|----------------|
| **AI Plant Scanner** | Take a photo of any plant, get instant identification with confidence % | "Don't know your plant's name? Snap and know in seconds" |
| **Cairo Weather Integration** | Real-time weather from Apple WeatherKit adjusts care tips | "Your plant care changes with Cairo's weather — automatically" |
| **Seasonal Care Matrix** | 12-row care guide: season x temperature x humidity x UV | "Summer in Cairo hits 45°C. We adjust your watering for that." |
| **My Garden Collection** | Save plants, track watering history, set reminders | "Your digital garden — never forget to water again" |
| **Smart Reminders** | Local push notifications for watering, fertilizing, pruning | "Lotus reminds you before your plant reminds you (by dying)" |
| **Egyptian Arabic** | Full app in colloquial Egyptian Arabic, not formal MSA | "Finally, plant care in your language — مش لغة كتب" |
| **RTL-Native Design** | Arabic isn't an afterthought — designed for it from day one | "Feels like home, reads like home" |
| **Placement Scoring** | Rate your plant's spot (1-5 stars) based on room + window direction | "Is your balcony killing your fern? We'll tell you." |
| **Guest Mode** | Scan unlimited plants without signing up | "Try before you commit — just like adopting a plant" |
| **Care History** | Full timeline of watering, fertilizing, pruning, repotting | "Track every drop of water, every grain of fertilizer" |

### Cairo-Specific Intelligence

- **Summer heat alerts** (>35°C): Increase watering 50%, bring plants indoors
- **Low humidity warnings**: Mist tropical plants weekly
- **Dust storm awareness**: Protect sensitive plants during khamaseen
- **AC management**: Tips for apartments with heavy air conditioning
- **Winter cold snaps** (<15°C): Reduce watering 20%, protect outdoor plants
- **Egyptian directions**: بحري (north), قبلي (south), شرق (east), غرب (west)

## 3. Brand Identity

### Color Palette

| Color | Hex | Name | Usage |
|-------|-----|------|-------|
| 🟢 | `#2D5F3F` | **Lotus Green** | Primary actions, icons, CTA buttons |
| 🔵 | `#4A90A4` | **Nile Blue** | Secondary accents, links |
| 🟡 | `#F7F3E9` | **Cairo Sand** | Background — warm, earthy, Egyptian |
| ⚫ | `#2C2C2C` | **Text Primary** | Headlines, body |
| ⚪ | `#FFFFFF` | **White** | Cards, surfaces |

### Design System

- **Golden Ratio (φ = 1.618)** — layouts use mathematical harmony
- **Fibonacci spacing** — 3, 5, 8, 13, 21, 34, 55, 89px gaps
- **Font**: Plus Jakarta Sans (clean, modern, bilingual-friendly)
- **Arabic calligraphy fonts**: Tharwat Emara Ruqaa, Decoetype Naskh Swashes
- **Corners**: Soft, rounded — nature-inspired, approachable
- **Shadows**: Subtle, iOS-native elevation
- **Orientation**: Portrait only
- **Theme**: Light mode only (Cairo Sand warmth)

### Visual Mood

Think: **warm desert light filtering through a green balcony**. The palette bridges Egypt's sandy landscape with the lush greens of home plants. Not clinical or tech-heavy — organic, warm, and inviting. Every layout breathes using golden ratio proportions, the same mathematical harmony found in nature (sunflower spirals, leaf arrangements).

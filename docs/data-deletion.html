Data Deletion Instructions for Lotus Plant Care

Last Updated: November 9, 2025

How to Delete Your Data 🗑️

We respect your right to delete your data from Lotus Plant Care. This page explains how to delete your account and all associated data.

Quick Delete (In-App)

Step 1: Open Settings

  1. Open the Lotus app
  2. Tap the hamburger menu (☰) in the top left
  3. Select "Settings"

Step 2: Delete Account

  1. Scroll to the bottom of the Settings page
  2. Tap "Delete Account"
  3. Confirm deletion when prompted
  4. Your account and all data will be permanently deleted

⚠️ Warning: This action is irreversible. All your plants, photos, and care logs will be permanently deleted.

Request Deletion via Email

If you can't access the app or prefer email:

Send a Deletion Request

  • Email: ahmedalgohari.rgb@gmail.com
  • Subject: "Data Deletion Request - Lotus Plant Care"

Include:

  • Your email address (the one you used to sign up)
  • Your account name (if you remember it)
  • Confirmation: "I request deletion of all my data from Lotus Plant Care"

Response Time: We'll confirm deletion within 48-72 hours.

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently remove:

✅ Account Data

  • Your email address
  • Your name and profile information
  • Your authentication tokens and sessions

✅ Plant Data

  • All plant profiles you created
  • All plant photos you uploaded
  • Plant nicknames, locations, and notes
  • All care logs (watering, fertilizing, pruning, repotting)

✅ Preferences

  • Language settings
  • Theme preferences
  • App preferences

✅ Storage

  • All images stored in Supabase Storage
  • All database entries associated with your account

What Happens After Deletion

Immediate Effects (Within 24 hours)

  • ✅ Account access revoked immediately
  • ✅ All data removed from active database
  • ✅ Photos deleted from Supabase Storage
  • ✅ Session tokens invalidated
  • ✅ You can no longer log in with your credentials

Complete Deletion (Within 30 days)

  • ✅ All backup copies removed
  • ✅ All cached data cleared
  • ✅ All database records permanently deleted
  • ✅ No trace of your account remains

Data We Cannot Delete

Some data may be retained for legal or technical reasons:

Legal Requirements

  • Transaction logs: If required by law (e.g., GDPR compliance logs)
  • Fraud prevention: Anonymous security logs (no personal identifiers)
  • Legal disputes: Data relevant to ongoing legal matters

Duration: Typically 30-90 days, unless legally required to retain longer.

Technical Limitations

  • Email unsubscribe records: To prevent re-adding you to email lists
  • Aggregated analytics: Anonymous, non-identifiable usage statistics

These do NOT contain personal information.

Third-Party Services

When you delete your account, we also:

Supabase (Database & Storage)

  • ✅ All database records deleted
  • ✅ All photos in Supabase Storage deleted
  • ✅ Row Level Security ensures no orphaned data

PlantNet API

  • ✅ No data stored (photos sent temporarily for identification only)
  • ✅ Nothing to delete

Google OAuth

  • ✅ OAuth tokens invalidated
  • ✅ Your Google account is unaffected
  • ℹ️ You may need to revoke app access in your Google account settings: myaccount.google.com/permissions

Facebook OAuth

  • ✅ OAuth tokens invalidated
  • ✅ Your Facebook account is unaffected
  • ℹ️ You may need to remove app access in Facebook settings: facebook.com/settings?tab=applications

Reactivation

Can I get my data back?

No. Deletion is permanent and irreversible.

Can I create a new account?

Yes! You can sign up again with:

  • The same email address
  • A different email address
  • Google, Facebook, or Apple sign-in

Important: Your new account starts fresh. We cannot restore deleted data.

Alternatives to Deletion

If you're not sure about deleting your account, consider:

1. Sign Out Temporarily

  • Just log out of the app
  • Your data remains saved
  • Log back in anytime

2. Delete Individual Plants

  • Delete specific plants instead of your entire account
  • Go to Plant Details → Delete Plant
  • Keeps your account but removes unwanted plants

3. Export Your Data First

  • Request a data export before deletion
  • Email us: ahmedalgohari.rgb@gmail.com
  • We'll send your data in JSON or CSV format
  • Then delete your account if you still want to

Guest Mode Data

If you used the app in Guest Mode (without creating an account):

  • ✅ All data stored locally on your device only
  • ✅ No data uploaded to our servers
  • ✅ Simply uninstall the app to delete all data
  • ✅ Or clear app data in your device settings

GDPR & Privacy Rights

If you're in the European Union, you have additional rights:

Right to Be Forgotten

  • You can request immediate deletion of all personal data
  • We comply within 30 days
  • Email us: ahmedalgohari.rgb@gmail.com with subject "GDPR Data Deletion Request"

Right to Data Portability

  • Request a copy of your data before deletion
  • We'll provide it in a machine-readable format (JSON/CSV)
  • Email us: ahmedalgohari.rgb@gmail.com with subject "GDPR Data Export Request"

Confirmation of Deletion

After we process your deletion request, we'll send a confirmation email to your registered email address.

What you'll receive:

  • ✅ Confirmation that your account has been deleted
  • ✅ Date and time of deletion
  • ✅ List of data types deleted
  • ✅ Reminder that deletion is permanent

If you don't receive confirmation within 72 hours, contact us.

Questions or Issues?

If you have trouble deleting your account or have questions:

We typically respond within 48-72 hours.

## Why This Theme Now

July 30 is peak Cairo summer. Air conditioning runs 24 hours a day in most Cairo apartments — creating a cold, bone-dry microclimate that confuses tropical plants and kills anything that needs humidity. This is distinctly Egyptian: the enemy isn't just 45°C heat outside, it's the 20°C AC cave inside. This carousel turns that frustration into educational content Lotus users will share because they've experienced it. Pillar 2 is also the most underused pillar recently (last run: June 8) — due for a fresh take.

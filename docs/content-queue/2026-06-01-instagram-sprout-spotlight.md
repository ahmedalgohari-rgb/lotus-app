## Plant Profile
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Common Name** | Silver Vase Plant |
| **Scientific Name** | *Aechmea fasciata* |
| **Arabic Name** | نبات الزهرية الفضية |
| **Difficulty** | Beginner |
| **Type** | Bromeliad |
| **Watering** | Every two weeks — keep central cup filled; water roots sparingly |
| **Light** | Bright indirect |
| **Cairo Suitability** | Excellent |
| **Summer Care** | Popular gift plant in Cairo. Long-lasting pink spike. Easy bromeliad care. |
| **Winter Care** | Keep cup filled. Watch for pups. Remove mother after pups grow. |
| **Optimal Temp** | 15–27°C — keep indoors during Cairo peak heat (AC room is perfect) |

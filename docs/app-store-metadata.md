## Description (4000 characters max)

Transform your plant care journey with Lotus – the intelligent plant companion that combines cutting-edge AI with beautiful design.

🌿 INSTANT PLANT IDENTIFICATION
Point your camera at any plant and discover its name, species, and care requirements in seconds. Powered by PlantNet's advanced botanical AI, Lotus identifies thousands of plant species with high accuracy.

💧 PERSONALIZED CARE SCHEDULES
Never forget to water again! Get customized watering, fertilizing, and pruning schedules tailored to each plant's specific needs. Track care history and receive timely reminders.

📸 BEAUTIFUL PLANT COLLECTION
Build and organize your personal plant library with photos, notes, and care logs. Watch your collection grow and thrive over time.

🌍 BILINGUAL SUPPORT
Full English and Arabic support with right-to-left (RTL) layouts for Arabic speakers. All plant names and care instructions available in both languages.

☁️ SECURE CLOUD SYNC
Your plant data syncs securely across devices via Supabase. Sign in with Google, Apple, or continue as a guest.

🔒 PRIVACY FIRST
We don't collect location data, contacts, or browsing history. Your plant photos and care logs are yours alone.

✨ KEY FEATURES:
• AI-powered plant identification (PlantNet API)
• Custom care schedules for watering, fertilizing, pruning
• Photo library with before/after tracking
• Care event logging and history
• Weather-aware recommendations for Cairo
• Guest mode (no account required for identification)
• Offline mode for saved plants

🌱 PERFECT FOR:
• Plant parents (beginners to experts)
• Gardeners and horticulturists
• Plant enthusiasts building collections
• Students learning botany
• Anyone who wants healthier, happier plants

📱 DESIGNED FOR iOS
Native iOS app with smooth animations, biometric authentication, and deep system integration. Optimized for all iPhone models from iPhone 12+.

🇪🇬 BUILT IN EGYPT
Created with love by Egyptian developers for plant lovers worldwide.

🆓 FREE TO USE
No subscription required to get started.

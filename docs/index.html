About Lotus

Lotus Plant Care is a free mobile app designed to help you take better care of your plants. We combine cutting-edge AI plant identification with personalized care recommendations, weather-based insights, and a comprehensive plant journal.

Key Features:

  • 🔍 AI Plant Identification - Identify plants instantly using your camera
  • 📅 Care Reminders - Never forget to water, fertilize, or prune your plants
  • 🌤️ Weather-Based Tips - Get care recommendations based on Cairo's weather
  • 📊 Plant Tracking - Monitor your plants' growth and care history
  • 🌍 Bilingual Support - Full English and Arabic support (RTL)

Technology & Security

Lotus Plant Care is built using modern, secure technologies:

  • React Native + Expo - Cross-platform mobile development
  • Supabase - Secure backend with Row Level Security
  • PlantNet API - AI-powered plant identification
  • OAuth 2.0 - Secure authentication with Google, Facebook, and Apple
  • HTTPS Encryption - All data transmitted securely

The app is open source and available on GitHub: github.com/ahmedalgohari-rgb/lotus-app

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Lotus Plant Care really free?

Yes! The app is completely free with no ads, no in-app purchases, and no hidden fees.

Do I need to create an account?

No! You can use the app in Guest Mode to try it out without creating an account. Create an account to save your plants long-term and sync across devices.

What data do you collect?

We collect your email, name (if you create an account), plant photos you upload, and care logs you create. We do NOT collect your location, contacts, or browsing history. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Can I delete my data?

Absolutely! You can delete individual plants, or delete your entire account at any time. See our Data Deletion Guide for instructions.

Is my data secure?

Yes! All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), stored securely on Supabase servers, and protected by Row Level Security to ensure you can only access YOUR data.

Do you support Arabic?

Yes! Lotus Plant Care has full Arabic (العربية) support with proper Right-to-Left (RTL) layout.

How accurate is plant identification?

Plant identification is provided by PlantNet's AI with 70-95% accuracy. For critical decisions (like edibility or toxicity), always verify with a professional botanist.

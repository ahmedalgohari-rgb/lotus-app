Data Deletion Instructions for Lotus Plant Care

Last Updated: November 9, 2025

Data Deletion Procedures

Users have the right to request deletion of their personal data and account from the Lotus Plant Care application. This document outlines the procedures for data deletion requests.

Method 1: In-Application Deletion

Step 1: Access Settings

  1. Launch the Lotus Plant Care application
  2. Navigate to the main menu (hamburger icon)
  3. Select "Settings"

Step 2: Initiate Account Deletion

  1. Scroll to the account management section
  2. Select "Delete Account"
  3. Confirm deletion when prompted
  4. Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted

Warning: Account deletion is irreversible. All plant data, photographs, care logs, and account information will be permanently removed.

Method 2: Email Deletion Request

If you are unable to access the application or prefer to request deletion via email:

Required Information

  • Recipient: ahmedalgohari.rgb@gmail.com
  • Subject Line: "Data Deletion Request - Lotus Plant Care"

Email Content Requirements

Your deletion request must include:

  • The email address associated with your Lotus Plant Care account
  • Your account name or user identifier (if known)
  • Explicit confirmation: "I request deletion of all my data from Lotus Plant Care"

Processing Time: Deletion requests submitted via email will be confirmed within 48-72 hours.

Data Subject to Deletion

Upon account deletion, the following data categories will be permanently removed:

Account Data

  • Email address and account credentials
  • User name and profile information
  • Authentication tokens and sessions

Plant Data

  • All plant profiles and entries
  • Uploaded plant photographs
  • Plant nicknames, locations, and descriptive information
  • Complete care log history (watering, fertilizing, pruning, repotting records)

Application Preferences

  • Language settings
  • Theme preferences
  • Application configuration data

Storage Data

  • All images stored in Supabase Storage infrastructure
  • All database records associated with the account

Deletion Timeline

Immediate Actions (Within 24 Hours)

  • Account access privileges revoked
  • Active database records removed
  • Photographs deleted from storage infrastructure
  • Authentication tokens invalidated
  • Login credentials rendered inoperative

Complete Deletion (Within 30 Days)

  • All backup copies removed from systems
  • Cached data cleared from temporary storage
  • Database records permanently expunged
  • No residual account traces remain in systems

Data Retention Exceptions

Certain data may be retained for limited periods due to legal or technical requirements:

Legal Requirements

  • Transaction Logs: Retained if required by applicable law (e.g., GDPR compliance logs)
  • Fraud Prevention: Anonymous security logs without personal identifiers
  • Legal Proceedings: Data relevant to ongoing or potential legal matters

Retention Duration: Typically 30-90 days, unless legally mandated for longer periods.

Technical Limitations

  • Unsubscribe Records: Maintained to prevent re-addition to communication lists
  • Aggregated Analytics: Anonymous, non-identifiable usage statistics

Note: These records contain no personally identifiable information.

Third-Party Service Data Deletion

Supabase (Database & Storage)

  • All database records permanently deleted
  • All photographs in Supabase Storage removed
  • Row Level Security ensures no orphaned data

PlantNet API

  • No persistent data storage (temporary identification processing only)
  • No deletion action required

Google OAuth

  • OAuth authentication tokens invalidated
  • Your Google account remains unaffected

Note: You may revoke application access independently through your Google account settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Facebook OAuth

  • OAuth authentication tokens invalidated
  • Your Facebook account remains unaffected

Note: You may remove application access independently through Facebook settings at facebook.com/settings?tab=applications.

Account Reactivation

Data Recovery

Not Available. Deleted data cannot be recovered. Account deletion is permanent and irreversible.

New Account Creation

Permitted. You may create a new account at any time using:

  • The same email address
  • A different email address
  • Google, Facebook, or Apple authentication

Important: New accounts begin with no data. Previously deleted information cannot be restored.

Alternatives to Account Deletion

If you are uncertain about permanently deleting your account, consider these alternatives:

1. Temporary Logout

  • Log out of the application
  • Account data remains preserved
  • Access can be restored at any time

2. Selective Data Deletion

  • Delete specific plant entries instead of entire account
  • Navigate to Plant Details and select "Delete Plant"
  • Account remains active with other data preserved

3. Data Export Prior to Deletion

  • Request data export before account deletion
  • Contact: ahmedalgohari.rgb@gmail.com
  • Data provided in JSON or CSV format
  • Proceed with deletion after receiving export

Guest Mode Data

For users utilizing Guest Mode (without account registration):

  • All data stored locally on device only
  • No data transmitted to servers
  • Complete data removal via application uninstallation
  • Alternative: Clear application data through device settings

GDPR Rights (European Union Users)

Users in the European Union have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

Right to be Forgotten

  • Request immediate deletion of all personal data
  • Compliance within 30 days
  • Contact: ahmedalgohari.rgb@gmail.com (Subject: "GDPR Data Deletion Request")

Right to Data Portability

  • Request complete data export prior to deletion
  • Machine-readable format provided (JSON/CSV)
  • Contact: ahmedalgohari.rgb@gmail.com (Subject: "GDPR Data Export Request")

Deletion Confirmation

Upon processing your deletion request, a confirmation will be sent to your registered email address containing:

  • Confirmation of account deletion completion
  • Date and time of deletion
  • List of data categories deleted
  • Reminder of deletion permanence

If confirmation is not received within 72 hours, please contact us.

Contact Information

For questions, issues, or additional assistance regarding data deletion:

  • Email: ahmedalgohari.rgb@gmail.com

Response Time: Inquiries typically receive responses within 48-72 hours.

Summary

Deletion Methods:

  2. Email request: ahmedalgohari.rgb@gmail.com

Data Deleted:
